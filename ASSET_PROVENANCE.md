# Ogre War asset provenance

Ogre War is an original project. This release candidate combines original Ogre War content with original Castlehold project assets that were intentionally reused and adapted as the game's art/audio foundation.

## Ogre War-authored content

- `assets/ogre_war_icon.png` and `assets/ogre_war_title.png`: generated specifically for Ogre War using OpenAI image generation.
- `assets/ui/phase10/menu_backdrop.png`, `loading_backdrop.png`, `ogre_war_logo.png`, `launcher_crest.png`, adaptive launcher layers and `boot_splash.png`: original Ogre War visual assets generated or composed for this release. The game UI draws buttons and labels in Godot for legibility at phone sizes.
- `assets/ui/phase10/humanitys_last_stand.ogv`: encoded from the original `Ogre_War_Opening_Cinematic.mp4` supplied by the user, preserving its audio and story; Ogg Theora/Vorbis is used for Godot playback.
- `assets/audio/*.wav`: original deterministic synthesized music/effects authored by `tools/generate_audio.py`; no downloaded samples or third-party recordings.
- `assets/ogre_modern/**`: Ogre War-authored fortress modules, equipment meshes, battlefield props, fortress-damage meshes, UI vectors and derived material maps. Phase 9 adds original hero-fortress overlays, battlefield-depth meshes, hero-unit equipment and recruit portrait SVGs generated by `tools/generate_phase9_art.py`.
- `scripts/**`: Ogre War gameplay, battle presentation, UI, VFX, projectile, fortress and adaptation code.

## Castlehold-derived content

`assets/castlehold/**` contains original Castlehold project assets reused with the user's permission as the base for Ogre War's current presentation, including rigged GLTF characters, surface materials, battle audio, music, synthesized defeat voices and environment resources. Ogre War adapts these assets with its own equipment, faction/age presentation, siege systems, fortress architecture and battle logic.

Full Castlehold provenance is preserved in `docs/CASTLEHOLD_ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
